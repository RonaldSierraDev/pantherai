Brainstorming session plan

1. Set the stage. Begin by explaining the purpose of the session and the importance of brainstorming in the app development process. Encourage open-mindedness and creativity.

2. Group formation. Divide students into small groups of four to five members. This size allows for diverse ideas while ensuring everyone can contribute.

3. Mind mapping. Have each group create a mind map starting with a central theme such as Health, Education or Entertainment. Encourage them to branch out with related ideas.

4. SCAMPER method. Introduce the SCAMPER technique to help students think critically about existing apps and how they can innovate on them.

5. Pitch preparation. Each group prepares a brief three to five minute pitch covering the problem, key features, target audience and unique selling points.

6. Gallery walk. After brainstorming, have groups post their ideas on a wall and let students walk around, read and add comments.
