PROMPT
I need to run a brainstorming session in my software engineering class to help student groups come up with app ideas.
Brainstorming session plan

1. Set the stage. Begin by explaining the purpose of the session and the importance of brainstorming in the app development process.

2. Mind mapping. Have each group create a mind map starting with a central theme such as Health, Education or Entertainment. 1 Encourage them to branch out with related ideas.

3. SCAMPER method. Introduce the SCAMPER technique to help students think critically about existing apps and how they can innovate on them.

4. Pitch preparation. Each group prepares a brief three to five minute pitch covering the problem, key features, target audience and unique selling points. 2

5. Next steps. Discuss how they can move forward with their ideas, including research, prototyping and development phases.
